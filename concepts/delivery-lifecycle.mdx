# Delivery lifecycle

Atlas Relay accepts an event, evaluates destination filters, and creates one delivery per matching destination. A delivery can move through `queued`, `attempting`, `delivered`, or `failed` states.

Temporary failures are retried according to the destination policy. See [delivery attempts](/reference/delivery-attempts) for the fields recorded for every request and response.
